## About

I am a PhD candidate in Stratigraphy and Palaeontology at the China University of Geosciences (Beijing). I was a visiting researcher at the University of Bristol from September 2023 to December 2024 and returned for a second research visit from April to October 2026, working with the Paleo Modelling Group.

My research combines deep-time climate modelling with palaeoclimate proxy evidence to investigate past climate systems and their evolution. My research interests include reconstructing global palaeo-Köppen climate zones throughout the Mesozoic and Cenozoic, assessing the climatic impacts of mountain uplift and palaeogeographic changes, and using climate-sensitive sediments and stable isotope geochemistry, particularly oxygen isotopes in calcareous nodules, to constrain past climate conditions.
