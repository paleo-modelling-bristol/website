## About

I am a visiting pgr student at the Paleo Modelling Group, from the School of Geography and Ocean Science at Nanjing University, supervised by Prof. Dan Lunt and Prof. Huayu Lu. My current research focuses on paleoclimate variations in east Asia during Eocene in orbital scale, combining model simulations and the early Eocene climate records from central China.
