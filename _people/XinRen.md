## About

I am a postdoc with the Paleo Modelling Group and the Marine Ecology and Climate (MEC) group. 
My research focuses on climate dynamics during warm geological periods, such as the Oligocene and Pliocene. 
I integrate Earth System Models, machine learning, and marine biogeochemical modeling to understand past climate mechanisms, with a broader interest in future climate prediction.

## Education

- PhD in Geographical Sciences, University of Bristol. 2020 - 2025
