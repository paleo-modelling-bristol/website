## About

I am a first-year PhD student in the School of Earth Sciences at the University of Bristol, supervised by Prof. Daniela Schmidt and Prof. Dan Lunt. My current research focuses on the vertical structure of marine ecosystem responses to climate change over the Cenozoic, combining fossil data, quantitative trait analysis, and palaeoclimate modelling to investigate how marine organisms respond to environmental change across different depths and timescales.

## Education

- BSc in Geology (Life Evolution and Environment), Nanjing University.
